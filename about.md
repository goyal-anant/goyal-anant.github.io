Anant is a human, a friend, a brother, and a son who likes to speak in the third person. He adores elephants, cats, and dogs. He is surprised by the fact that the more knowledge the human race gains, the more they exploit other species and ultimately themselves. He likes to talk about science, AI, human relations, philosophy, music, and writing, among many other things. He likes minimalism doped with traditional design. He spends most of his time building things which is super easy now with AI. You think, you build!

He believes the best way to break ice with someone is to talk about their food choices or travel ideas. He likes raw people, and lives by a simple philosophy from Lao Tzu: *Seek not, find.*

He constantly searched for happiness for about twenty years until he learned that there is no such thing called happiness, there is only misery, less and more; you need to choose the path of less misery to be so called *happy*.
