Anant is a human, a friend, a brother, and a son who likes to speak in the third person. At present, he is earning PhD at [NEMO](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/uday/nemo.html), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, India, where he works on intelligent wireless power transfer systems, inverse design of metasurfaces, and computational electromagnetics with [Prof. Uday Khankhoje](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/uday/).

He likes to talk about science, AI, human relations, philosophy, music, and writing, among many other things. He likes minimalism doped with traditional design. He spends most of his time building things, which is super easy now with AI: you think, you build!

He adores elephants, cats, and dogs, and is surprised that the more knowledge the human race gains, the more it exploits other species, and ultimately itself.

He believes the best way to break ice with someone is to talk about their food choices or travel ideas. He likes raw people, and lives by a simple philosophy from Lao Tzu: *Seek not, find.*

He constantly searched for happiness for about twenty years until he learned there is no such thing as happiness, existence is misery, less or more; you choose the path of less misery to be so-called *happy*.

Say Hi to him! The best way to reach out is by dropping an email at [anantnug[at]gmail[dot]com](mailto:anantnug@gmail.com).

PS: Now he is searching for ways to minimize animal cruelty without becoming irrational.
