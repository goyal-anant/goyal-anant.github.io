Anant is a human, a friend, a brother, and a son who mostly speaks in the third person. He adores elephants and cats. He is surprised by the fact that the more knowledge the human race gains, the more they exploit themselves. He likes to talk about science, AI, human relations, philosophy, music, and writing, among many other things. He likes minimalism doped with traditional design. He spends most of his time building things, and now with AI, not even the sky is the limit.

He likes raw people and avoids those who appear to be something they are not. He lives by a simple yet powerful philosophy from Lao Tzu: *Seek not, find.*
