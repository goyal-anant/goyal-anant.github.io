It is difficult to detach *I* from existence, but to experiment, I will try to speak in the third person. It is important because only by doing that one may truly talk about themselves.

Anant is a human, a friend, a brother, and a son who enjoys the company of his thoughts. At present, he is earning PhD at [NEMO](https://www.ee.iitm.ac.in/uday/nemo.html), Indian Institute of Technology Madras, India, where he works on intelligent wireless power transfer systems, inverse design of metasurfaces, and computational electromagnetics with Prof. Uday Khankhoje.

He likes to talk about science, AI, human relations, philosophy, music, and writing, among many other things. He appreciates minimalism doped with traditional design. He spends most of his time building things: you think, you build!

He adores elephants, cats, and dogs, and is surprised that the more knowledge the human race gains, the more it exploits other species, and ultimately itself.

He believes the best way to break ice with someone is to talk about good food or travel ideas after you are done with the regular 'hello/hi' and 'what do you do?' stuff. He likes raw people, and lives by a philosophy from Lao Tzu: *Seek not, find!*

He constantly searched for happiness for about twenty years until he learned there is no such thing as happiness, existence is misery, less or more; you choose the path of less misery to be so-called *happy*.

Wanna say *hi* to him? The best way to reach out is by dropping an email at [anantnug[at]gmail[dot]com](mailto:anantnug@gmail.com).

PS: He is currently searching for ways to minimize animal cruelty without becoming irrational.
